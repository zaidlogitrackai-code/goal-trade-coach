- Whether new risk may be taken comes only from pure `src/lib/circuit-breaker.ts` (`evaluateBreaker`, losses only, after drawdown-circuit-breaker; rules = the circuit_breaks CHECK): realized P&L for the day / Monday week / month in trading_settings.timezone (closes by closed_at; a partial-close trim by its own `at` and the rest of the trade at close, because trades.pnl is the whole trade), % limits anchored by `anchorBalance` on the day's start balance (else live), the losing streak by close time (break-even is a win, COOLDOWN until last loss + streak_cooldown_hours), manual pause/cooldown and latched circuit_breaks rows; the strictest state wins, every triggered rule is kept with its release time, and broken rows after breaker_since (null pnl, bad close time, a duplicate pair, dropped/corrupt ledger) make it PARTIAL = HALTED, while older ones and unaccepted survival defaults only warn; breaker_since null = preview. Max trades and the profit cap are not breaker rules but the `protectProfit` warning in `src/lib/trading-status.ts`, which otherwise only maps the day's display status and tone for the dashboard and trade form (it still drives them until the screens switch to the breaker); copy and AI facts come from `breakerCopy`/`breakerForPrompt` — code decides who may trade, a stale or edited row can't quietly lift a halt.
- Whether to enter one trade comes only from pure `src/lib/pre-trade-gate.ts` (`evaluatePreTrade`, after pre-trade-discipline-gate): GO < NO_ACTIONABLE < REVIEW_REQUIRED < NO_GO, every reason kept and the decision only rises. NO_GO: the breaker not ALLOWED (PARTIAL is HALTED; in preview it only asks for a review), no stop, contradicting levels, actual risk (the larger of typed and `riskUsd`) above `plannedRisk` (today's plan, never looser than max_risk_per_trade), an explicit «لا», self-declared revenge, a loss (close or partial trim, `lastLossWithin`) inside `REVENGE_WINDOW_MIN` before the entry, a rest day. REVIEW_REQUIRED: a blank answer (owner decision 5), unknown risk, no plan, `protectProfit`, outside session hours (both set), a close after reviews_since not yet reviewed (null = off), missing settings or breaker. historical/watch are NO_ACTIONABLE; temporary rules, heat and a sensitive emotion only warn. `applyAiOpinion` can only raise to REVIEW, `gateRow` is the pre_trade_checks row, and trade-gate.ts only measures (R:R, risk %, room left) — one decision for the form, the stepper and AI-logged trades, and saving is never blocked.
- What to do now comes only from pure `src/lib/day-pipeline.ts`: `pipelineState` turns the rows into facts through the engines that own them (the breaker, the gate's `unreviewedCloses` after reviews_since, `protectProfit`), and `dayPipeline` picks one of seven steps (0 الإعداد … 6 مراجعة الأسبوع) in a fixed order — setup → 0, a session left open from an earlier day → 1, PARTIAL → 1 (repair), last week due → 6, HALTED/COOLDOWN → 1, an open trade with no stop → 4 (danger), an unreviewed close → 4, no plan → 2, plan or protect-profit reached → 5 with «صفقة جديدة» hidden, a rest day → 5, else 3 — with one main button, every blocker kept, and `isBlocked` = breaker not TRADING_ALLOWED. The dashboard (`day-flow.ts`), the nav dot (`journey.ts`, `usePipeline`) and سَنَد (`pipelineForPrompt`, quoted, never re-derived) are thin adapters over it — the Today card, the nav and the AI can't point at different steps.
- How a trade went comes only from pure `src/lib/trade-review.ts` (`reviewTrade`, after trade-performance-coach), judged at its entry: process (an active latched break or a NO_GO he entered on = market_regime_gate, once — a reason another rule judges, his «لا» or the size, is that rule's; an explicit «لا» on plan/stop/size, no stop, REVIEW steps he skipped, a violation logged on it, no notes/analysis/plan before entry), risk (size vs `plannedRisk` >1×/>1.25×, heat vs the day's room, 3/4 losses before, the week within 10% of its limit, over plan right after a same-day winner), execution (an unplanned stop move — wider is critical —, a fear exit under 0.8× the planned R:R, adding to a loser) and his own words (folded-Arabic regexes, negations and the app's revenge line skipped). A missing number (no answers, legacy/backfill risk 0, risk_source unknown) is `unclear` and only gives `warn`; cooldown (≥2 criticals, or revenge after ≥2 losses) > violation > review > warn > ok; root cause his postmortem first; tags never empty; next rules are time-boxed cards that `acceptRule` puts in rules_today and `activeTempRules` reads back for the gate — one verdict for the review sheet, patterns and the AI, which can only raise it (`stricterVerdict`).
- What keeps repeating over a week (Monday, in trading_settings.timezone) or a month comes only from pure `src/lib/patterns.ts` (`periodReview`, after trade-performance-coach): trades by close time with their stored trade_reviews, tag counts with the trend against up to 8 earlier periods that had reviewed trades (a week off proves nothing), recurring (≥2) and rule drift (one behaviour tag ≥3; no_pattern_detected/unknown_size never drift), clean (ok/warn) vs rule-breaking P&L, quadrants, violations by rule and source, the gate's saves (NO_GO with proceeded=false and no trade), entries on a NO_GO, watched-not-taken, the analyst's hit-rate by confidence (trades taken in the card's direction), whether last period's accepted rule held, and 1-2 time-boxed next rules. One mistake is one event (a violation row, the NO_GO he entered on and the trade's own violation verdict), and 2 events within 7 days make the period cooldown; `periodRow` is the weekly/monthly trade_reviews row and `patternsForPrompt` (≤4 weeks + the last month) the AI's copy — the week card, the month card and سَنَد can't count a pattern two ways.
- Every "today / this week (from Monday) / this month" boundary, day key and reminder time comes only from pure `src/lib/trading-day.ts`, in trading_settings.timezone (safeTz falls back to Asia/Riyadh) — the server runs in UTC and the phone can travel, so limits, alerts, plans and once-a-day locks must share the trader's own calendar.
- Typed numbers go through `parseDecimal` (`src/lib/num.ts`, used by NumberField) and money at risk only through `riskUsd`/contract size in `src/lib/trade-idea.ts` — an Arabic-region iPhone types '١٫٠٨', and a raw price distance is not dollars.
- Emotion words and the revenge window come only from pure `src/lib/emotions.ts`: `REVENGE_WINDOW_MIN(settings)` = max(30, cooldown_minutes), checked with `inRevengeWindow`, in the discipline score, focus insights and (later) the gate — they must agree on what "right after a loss" means.
- Symbols are read from free text only by `detectSymbols` (`src/lib/market-symbols.ts`): Arabic aliases match whole words (optional و/ب/ل/ال prefixes) after normalising alef/ة/tatweel, and there is no bare 'ين' — it hides inside وين/الحين/بين and pulled USDJPY charts into gold analyses.
- App data is read/written from the browser Supabase client under owner-only RLS; protected pages live under `src/routes/_authenticated/` (ssr: false) — single-user personal app, no server functions needed yet.
- AI features (analyst, coach, report summary) run only through authenticated server functions in `src/lib/ai.functions.ts`, using `src/lib/ai.server.ts` (gateway call, streamed to final text; an incomplete reply is retried once and never passed on, usage is logged, a per-instance daily cap `AI_DAILY_CAP` stops runaway loops; voice notes via `transcribe`, 30s timeout). What every agent knows comes only from `src/lib/ai-context.ts` `loadContextData` (one Promise.all, paged, errors thrown, %-limits on the live balance) and pure `src/lib/ai-context-format.ts` `formatContext(data, mode)` (agent | brief caps): breaker → pipeline step → «حالة اليوم (محسوبة، لا تعيد حسابها)» → gate decisions and the last checks → «آخر خسارة قبل N دقيقة» → reviews and tag counts → patterns (4 weeks + month) → violations by rule → the last session reviews → one line per trade with his notes/close_notes → today's ids, then strategies (markets, timeframes), rules, examples, notes and profile — quoted by the model, never re-derived, so «كم باقي لي اليوم؟» matches the Today card. The agent schema returns reply, actions, suggestions and step.
- Discipline numbers (score, reasons, revenge and quick re-entries, emotion vs result, today's nudges) come only from pure `src/lib/discipline.ts` `disciplineReport` (last 30 days, or the reports page's `range`; the cooldown nudge counts today's closes only and offers the trader's own `cooldownMinutes`, never rewriting it); `src/lib/stats.ts` keeps only performance helpers (tradeStats, groupPnl, equityCurve) — the discipline page, reports and AI can't show two scores.
- Risk settings are judged only by pure `src/lib/risk-validation.ts` `validateRiskSettings` (the limits form, the summary and update_settings, which blocks only errors its change creates): finite non-negative numbers, whole trade count and streak, daily ≤ weekly ≤ monthly loss limits (targets only warn), limits within the balance, session hours both-or-neither and HH:MM (start after end = over midnight, as the gate reads it), and an ISO-4217 currency (`normalizeCurrency`: USDT/USDC are booked as USD with a note); update_settings drops null/'' keys before coercion, takes only a currency enum and HH:MM session hours. Money is shown only through `fmtMoney` (`src/lib/money.ts`, re-exported by queries.ts; `trim` for cards), which falls back to "1,234.5 CODE" for a code Intl rejects — a coerced null was 0 = a limit switched off, and a saved "USDT" threw on every money screen.
- What سَنَد and المحلل may propose comes only from pure `src/lib/agent-action-spec.ts` (types, zod schemas, labels, each action's class JOURNAL/STATE/DESTRUCTIVE, and `allowedActions(mode)` for analyst/setup/day/manager: the model's schema enum is built per mode and coachReply's `screenActions` keeps only the mode's types on the server), and whether one runs by itself only from pure `src/lib/action-policy.ts` `needsApproval` (asked in auto mode; `agent-actions.ts` only loads its facts and writes the rows in the browser). DESTRUCTIVE always waits; JOURNAL and STATE run by themselves, on a stopped day too (owner decision 3), unless a STATE change loosens something against the limits in force (limits_pct on `anchorBalance`, so stale dollars on file can't pass a raise; a failed read waits): a limit, target or the profit cap raised or switched off, a bigger starting balance, a shorter cooldown_minutes/streak_cooldown_hours, a new time zone, today's plan raised after a trade, a closed trade's result edited, a stop widened or removed, set_balance or a withdrawal, a rule switched off, a banned strategy allowed again. A trade the AI logs that is already placed is JOURNAL even on a NO_GO or a stopped day (owner decision 4: a waiting card would hide it from the breaker; `askReason` flags it for the violation and سَنَد's question); only a pre_entry ticket waits on a stopped day, a NO_GO or a market off his list — full control without silent data loss or a quietly loosened limit.
