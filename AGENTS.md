- Whether new risk may be taken comes only from pure `src/lib/circuit-breaker.ts` (`evaluateBreaker`, losses only, after drawdown-circuit-breaker; rules = the circuit_breaks CHECK): realized P&L for the day / Monday week / month in trading_settings.timezone (closes by closed_at; a partial-close trim by its own `at` and the rest of the trade at close, because trades.pnl is the whole trade), % limits anchored by `anchorBalance` on the day's start balance (else live), the losing streak by close time (break-even is a win, COOLDOWN until last loss + streak_cooldown_hours), manual pause/cooldown and latched circuit_breaks rows; the strictest state wins, every triggered rule is kept with its release time, and broken rows after breaker_since (null pnl, bad close time, a duplicate pair, dropped/corrupt ledger) make it PARTIAL = HALTED, while older ones and unaccepted survival defaults only warn; breaker_since null = preview. Max trades and the profit cap are not breaker rules but the `protectProfit` warning in `src/lib/trading-status.ts`, which otherwise only maps the day's display status and tone for the dashboard and trade form (it still drives them until the screens switch to the breaker); copy and AI facts come from `breakerCopy`/`breakerForPrompt` — code decides who may trade, a stale or edited row can't quietly lift a halt.
- Whether to enter one trade comes only from pure `src/lib/pre-trade-gate.ts` (`evaluatePreTrade`, after pre-trade-discipline-gate): GO < NO_ACTIONABLE < REVIEW_REQUIRED < NO_GO, every reason kept and the decision only rises. NO_GO: the breaker not ALLOWED (PARTIAL is HALTED; in preview it only asks for a review), no stop, contradicting levels, actual risk (the larger of typed and `riskUsd`) above `plannedRisk` (today's plan, never looser than max_risk_per_trade), an explicit «لا», self-declared revenge, a loss (close or partial trim, `lastLossWithin`) inside `REVENGE_WINDOW_MIN` before the entry, a rest day. REVIEW_REQUIRED: a blank answer (owner decision 5), unknown risk, no plan, `protectProfit`, outside session hours (both set), a close after reviews_since not yet reviewed (null = off), missing settings or breaker. historical/watch are NO_ACTIONABLE; temporary rules, heat and a sensitive emotion only warn. `applyAiOpinion` can only raise to REVIEW, `gateRow` is the pre_trade_checks row, and trade-gate.ts only measures (R:R, risk %, room left) — one decision for the form, the stepper and AI-logged trades, and saving is never blocked.
- What to do now comes only from pure `src/lib/day-pipeline.ts`: `pipelineState` turns the rows into facts through the engines that own them (the breaker, the gate's `unreviewedCloses` after reviews_since, `protectProfit`), and `dayPipeline` picks one of seven steps (0 الإعداد … 6 مراجعة الأسبوع) in a fixed order — setup → 0, a session left open from an earlier day → 1, PARTIAL → 1 (repair), last week due → 6, HALTED/COOLDOWN → 1, an open trade with no stop → 4 (danger), an unreviewed close → 4, no plan → 2, plan or protect-profit reached → 5 with «صفقة جديدة» hidden, a rest day → 5, else 3 — with one main button, every blocker kept, and `isBlocked` = breaker not TRADING_ALLOWED. The dashboard (`day-flow.ts`), the nav dot (`journey.ts`, `usePipeline`) and سَنَد (`pipelineForPrompt`, quoted, never re-derived) are thin adapters over it — the Today card, the nav and the AI can't point at different steps.
- Every "today / this week (from Monday) / this month" boundary, day key and reminder time comes only from pure `src/lib/trading-day.ts`, in trading_settings.timezone (safeTz falls back to Asia/Riyadh) — the server runs in UTC and the phone can travel, so limits, alerts, plans and once-a-day locks must share the trader's own calendar.
- Typed numbers go through `parseDecimal` (`src/lib/num.ts`, used by NumberField) and money at risk only through `riskUsd`/contract size in `src/lib/trade-idea.ts` — an Arabic-region iPhone types '١٫٠٨', and a raw price distance is not dollars.
- Emotion words and the revenge window come only from pure `src/lib/emotions.ts`: `REVENGE_WINDOW_MIN(settings)` = max(30, cooldown_minutes), checked with `inRevengeWindow`, in the discipline score, focus insights and (later) the gate — they must agree on what "right after a loss" means.
- Symbols are read from free text only by `detectSymbols` (`src/lib/market-symbols.ts`): Arabic aliases match whole words (optional و/ب/ل/ال prefixes) after normalising alef/ة/tatweel, and there is no bare 'ين' — it hides inside وين/الحين/بين and pulled USDJPY charts into gold analyses.
- App data is read/written from the browser Supabase client under owner-only RLS; protected pages live under `src/routes/_authenticated/` (ssr: false) — single-user personal app, no server functions needed yet.
- AI features (analyst, coach, report summary) run only through authenticated server functions in `src/lib/ai.functions.ts`, using `src/lib/ai.server.ts` (gateway call, streamed to final text) — keeps the API key server-side and grounds replies in the user's own data.
- Performance/discipline/report numbers come from pure helpers in `src/lib/stats.ts` — one calculation shared by every page.
- AI-proposed changes are structured actions (`src/lib/agent-actions.ts`) validated with zod and executed in the browser; سَنَد auto-runs them except DESTRUCTIVE actions (deletes) and any loosening of a limit, which always wait for a click (owner decision, docs/decisions/sanad-steps.md); `loosenedLimits` is judged against the limits in force (% of the live balance, not the stale dollars on file) and counts a time-zone change, since it moves "today" — full control without silent data loss.
- The daily morning message is generated lazily on the first visit of the day (`morningGreeting`, unique per user/day) — no scheduler needed for a single-user app.
- Navigation comes only from `src/lib/nav-registry.ts` ({path, title, label, icon, group, tabRoot}, a Record over the router's paths, so a new route fails tsc until it is listed): four fixed tabs plus a «المزيد» sheet grouped in step order (اليوم → المراجعة → الإعداد → النظام), the desktop sidebar, and the phone's iOS nav bar (large title; RTL back chevron = history back, else `backTarget` → /dashboard; then the breaker-chip slot, bell and gear). Nothing reorders (planNav only dots the current step's page where it sits); agent chats have one `ChatHeader`, and page titles are screen-reader-only on the phone because the nav bar shows them; chat switches replace history — stable positions, every route reachable, no double headers, and back leaves the page.
- AI-created trades are de-duplicated via `src/lib/trade-dedupe.ts` (symbol+direction+entry, 24h) and count as closed only by status 'closed' or an exit price (`isClosedUpload` in agent-actions.ts; an explicit 'open' wins) — re-uploading the same broker screenshot must never double-count, and floating P&L on an MT5 Trade tab is not a result.
- Bulk reads that feed money numbers (trades, closed P&L) page through pure `src/lib/fetch-all.ts` and throw on error; the capital ledger is read only via `readCapitalRow` (queries.ts, oldest row, warns on duplicates) — PostgREST silently caps a response at 1000 rows, and a balance missing its losses loosens every % limit.
- Risk alerts come only from pure `src/lib/risk-watch.ts`, run client-side by `RiskWatcher` on every heartbeat beat (each visible minute, and at once on resume; settings and today's plan from the query cache via fetchQuery) and de-duplicated via `notifications.dedupe_key` (an insert's 23505 means already delivered; any other error is logged and toasted, and a reminder only advances once delivered) — in-app only (user chose no push), one collapsed toast per run, so alerts never repeat or vanish.
- Risk management page is a chat with the risk guardian (shared SessionAgent with title/mode props, thread "حارس المخاطر"; coachReply mode=risk adds RISK_MODE); daily morning note via riskMorning deduped by notifications.dedupe_key; RiskWatcher also posts alerts into that thread — one agent chat per area keeps everything conversational.
- Discipline streak = login streak (user_visits, one row per day written silently by VisitRecorder) computed only in pure src/lib/streak.ts loginStreak (Mon–Fri, tz-aware); shown only on the discipline page and in AI context.
- Performance dashboard metrics (period windows, drawdown, R, streaks, calendar) come only from pure `src/lib/perf.ts` (tested) — keeps the numbers tz-correct and consistent.
- Each area (risk, strategies, discipline, analyst) is a dedicated agent chat thread via SessionAgent + coachReply mode (risk|strategy), hidden from the coach thread list — keeps everything conversational.
- Daily plan (target/trades/stop/max loss) lives in daily_plans (one row per day, revised_count tracks mid-day changes); greed/breach checks only in pure src/lib/daily-plan.ts — the discipline agent reads them from context.
- The agent persona is named سَنَد; every delete action is listed in DESTRUCTIVE so it always waits for its own click (never auto-run, never part of «وافق على الكل») — prevents accidental data loss in auto mode.
- Appearance (فاتح | داكن | تلقائي) resolves only in pure `src/lib/theme.ts`; the html `light` class is the one runtime truth (`dark:` = not light), set before first paint by `THEME_BOOT_SCRIPT` (generated from theme.ts, also follows the iPhone live), with meta theme-color and Sonner following it — no theme flash, and status bar, toasts and page never disagree.
- Phone UI goes through shared pieces: sheets via `ResponsiveSheet` (vaul drawer under 768px, Dialog above; a `<form>` body when it saves), every "are you sure" via `ConfirmDialog` (never window.confirm/prompt), conversation deletes via `DeleteThreadButton` (`deleteWithUndo`: «تراجع» window, commits when the app is left), bell rows route only via pure `src/lib/notification-target.ts`; Button/Input/Checkbox get 44pt floors under `pointer-coarse:` and `hover:` only fires on hover devices — iPhone-first: native sheets, no English browser prompts, no data lost to a stray tap.
- Every agent chat (coach included) is `SessionAgent` inside an `agent-page` frame sized from `--vvh`: `useVisualViewport` (mounted once in AppShell) sets `--vvh` and `html[data-kb]` (tab bar hidden) via pure `keyboardOpen`, SessionAgent locks page scroll (`html[data-chat]`) and scrolls only its own list, following new rows only while pinned to the bottom (`shouldAutoScroll`, «جديد ↓» otherwise); Return and paste rules come only from pure `src/lib/chat-ui.ts` (Return is a newline on touch, an image is taken only from a paste into the composer) — the composer stays on the iPhone keyboard and every agent gets each chat fix at once.
- Chat photos are decoded at their reduced size (`targetSize` in `src/lib/image.ts`: createImageBitmap resize where honoured, else an <img> into a capped canvas), and AI markdown tables come from pure `src/lib/md-tables.ts` (no remark-gfm: no new dependencies) — a 48MP iPhone photo decoded in full fails on the phone, and the analyst answers watchlists with tables.
- Freshness comes only from one heartbeat (pure `src/lib/heartbeat.ts`, driven by `useHeartbeat` in `src/hooks/use-resume.ts`, mounted once in the signed-in layout; components join via `useBeat`): each visible minute it re-reads trades and the bell, runs RiskWatcher and fetches only the newer rows of the open chat (`appendNewer`); back after >15s away/offline or on a new trading day it re-reads trades, settings, notifications, daily plan, sessions and the open thread. No refetchInterval or polling setInterval anywhere; query defaults staleTime 60s, no focus/reconnect refetch; settings re-read only when trades/capital data really changed; uid() reads the stored session; browser storage only through `src/lib/safe-storage.ts`; the signed-in layout shows a retry screen (never onboarding) while settings can't load; a stale chunk after a deploy reloads once; backdrop blur only ≥768px — an iPhone home-screen app is frozen in the background and must be right the moment it returns, at ≤4 requests per idle minute.
- Live market data for the nine instruments comes only from `src/lib/market.server.ts`: TradingView public scanner for price/indicators (matches the chart), Yahoo candles shifted onto that price for structure; analyst-only, no on-screen ticker — free, no key.
- Notifications are hidden (`dismissed`), never deleted — by the bell's clear buttons and سَنَد's delete_notifications; the bell filters to today in its SELECT — the rows double as once-only locks (`dedupe_key`/`day`) for alerts and the daily AI messages, so deleting them regenerates duplicates.
- Chat sends go through `src/lib/chat-retry.ts` (`sendReliably`: on a network drop it watches the thread for the reply, resends once only if the message never arrived; coachReply runs its AI work under `keepAlive` (waitUntil via `src/lib/request-context.server.ts`, set in `src/server.ts`) so replies finish after a disconnect) and store only a small thumbnail in `coach_messages.image` — phone connections drop and must never lose or duplicate a message.
- Strategy training examples live in strategy_examples and are injected into every agent's context via buildContext; training progress score only in pure src/lib/training.ts — one learned profile shared by all agents.
- Money limits are stored also as % of balance (trading_settings.limits_pct) and turned into dollars only by src/lib/effective-limits.ts using the live capital balance — limits must follow deposits/corrections/P&L.
- Analyst paper self-test (10-min virtual scalps) runs client-side in `paper-test-panel.tsx` with pure `src/lib/paper-test.ts`; results go only into the analyst chat, never the trades table — keeps real stats clean.
